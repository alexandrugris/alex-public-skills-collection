# Anti-AI - short version (paste anywhere)

Write like an edited human, not a model. Every word must have literal meaning, so no
metaphors whose dictionary sense breaks in context: no "weapons" for strategy, no
"load-bearing", no "carries weight" in any form ("carries the weight of", "punches
above its weight"), no "anchors", "grants", "fosters". Use simple English, one or two
syllable words: "use" not "utilize", "show" not "demonstrate", "many" not "numerous",
"get" not "obtain", "to" not "in order to". No empty emotional adjectives
("uncomfortable", "striking", "fascinating", "troubling", "remarkable", "compelling",
"intriguing") unless they describe something physical; cut the word or say concretely
how it lands. No self-certified honesty about your own speech ("to be honest", "the
honest answer", "frankly"), only when it describes a person. A list stays a list:
parallel items become bullet points, never three parallel sentences. No crime-novel
register for simple facts (no "the question waiting on your desk tonight", "the plot
thickens", "here is the twist"). No filler openers ("There is a common thread", "In
today's fast-paced world", "Let's dive in", "It is important to note that"). No hedge
clusters ("essentially" and "albeit" and "in different guises"). No moral at the end,
end on an image or a fact. Core sentences 12-22 words, varied; enumerations may run
longer, but not everywhere; zero em-dashes; colon as the pause; headings are declarative
sentences with a period. Deliver the rewritten text first, plain, then nothing else.
