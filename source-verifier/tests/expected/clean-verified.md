# Holding environments need fair systems.

*A short verified note on leadership and reality testing.*

Two hundred people sit in the glass room while the projector hums. Nobody speaks when the restructuring slide appears.

## Holding and identity workspaces.

In their [2010 paper](https://gpetriglieri.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/01/identity_workspaces_AMLE_2010.pdf), Gianpiero Petriglieri and Jennifer Petriglieri define a "holding environment" as "a social context that reduces disturbing affect and facilitates sense making". In a [2018 study](https://doi.org/10.1177/0001839217720930), Petriglieri, Jennifer Petriglieri, and Jack Wood examine how mobile managers craft "portable selves".

## Reality testing and limits.

In [Prisoners of Leadership](https://flora.insead.edu/fichiersti_wp/Inseadwp1987/87-01.pdf) (1987), Manfred Kets de Vries examines how collusive relationships erode reality testing. In a [2026 critique](https://doi.org/10.1108/IJOA-04-2026-6860), Seth Allcorn warns against imposing mechanistic systems logic on unconscious defenses.
