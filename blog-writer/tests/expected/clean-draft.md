# A quiet pipeline

Small services fail in small ways, and small ways are cheap to watch. This sample
draft exists as the positive control for `validate_draft.py`: it passes the gate
without warnings.

The material is deliberately thin. It makes one first-person observation, links one
external source for one external fact, and keeps the rest to what a person can see
on their own machine. The scanner reads it clean because every sentence says only
what it can back.
